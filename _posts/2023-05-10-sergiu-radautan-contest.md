### 🥈 Main Award – 2nd Place: **DevHealth Patient Monitor**

Developed in collaboration with **Daniel Țîrche**, under the guidance of **Prof. Adrian Bîrnaz**, the **DevHealth** system is a low-cost patient monitoring device based on **STM32F411CE** and **ESP32**. It integrates:

- ECG, SpO2, IR and environmental sensors (MQ3, MQ7, MQ135, SHT3x)
- TFT screen + encoder interface
- UART communication between STM32 and ESP32
- Web-based medical dashboard

📂 [View project on GitHub](https://github.com/Creciunel/patient_Monitor_STM411CE)

### 🛠️ About the Temperature Control Device

The second awarded system is an ESP32-based FreeRTOS application for precise thermal regulation, featuring:

- 220V galvanic isolation and BT138 control
- Cross-platform interface (web/Windows/Android)
- PID temperature stabilization with feedback
- Visualization tools and real-time status graphing

This was developed with **Gheorghe Creciunel**, coordinated by **Dr. Eduard Monaico**.
