### 🌍 About the CROSSCOMP Initiative

CROSSCOMP is a cross-border collaboration project between:

- **Technical University of Moldova (TUM)**  
- **Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași (TUIASI)**  
- **State Agrarian University of Moldova (UASM)**

The project aims to improve academic education and infrastructure in the **automotive engineering sector**, aligning regional curricula with modern industry requirements.

---

### 🧑‍💻 Participation Highlights

As a participant, I attended a dedicated **webinar series** held in April 2021, focusing on **modern trends in automotive engineering**, including:

- Hybrid and electric vehicle systems  
- Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS)  
- Embedded electronics and diagnostics  
- Curriculum innovation and modern teaching methods  
- Industry–academia collaboration case studies

The webinars were part of a broader training initiative that included lab upgrades, course improvements, summer schools, and master program planning under the name **Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (HEV)**.

### 🤝 Project Objectives Recap

- Upgrade university laboratories (TUM, TUIASI, UASM)

- Improve teaching methods and academic infrastructure
- Facilitate international student exchange and entrepreneurship
