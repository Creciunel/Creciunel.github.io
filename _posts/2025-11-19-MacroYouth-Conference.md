### 🏆 Award — 3rd Prize for Best Poster

I was awarded the **III Prize for Best Poster** for:

**PP15. Optical and structural characterization of Fe₃O₄ aeromaterial**  
*Cătălin Creciunel, Tatiana Galatonova, Vadim Morari, Florica Doroftei, Andrei Dascalu, Laura Ursu, Vladimir Ciobanu*

📄 Abstract in the Book of Abstracts, p. 87:  
[https://icmpp.ro/macroyouth2025/files/ProgramandBookofAbstractMacroYouth2025.pdf](https://icmpp.ro/macroyouth2025/files/ProgramandBookofAbstractMacroYouth2025.pdf)

---

### 🧩 Abstract (PP15)

> Magnetite-based aeromaterials (Fe₃O₄) represent a promising class of functional porous structures with potential applications in catalysis, adsorption, sensing, and energy storage.  
> In this work, we performed an **optical and structural characterization** of a Fe₃O₄ aeromaterial, correlating morphology, crystallinity, and optical response.  
> SEM imaging, XRD analysis, and UV-Vis spectroscopy confirm the high porosity and phase purity of the structure.  
> These findings highlight the potential of Fe₃O₄ aeromaterials in future nanotechnology applications.
