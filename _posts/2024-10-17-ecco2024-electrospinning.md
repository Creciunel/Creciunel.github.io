### ✨ Abstract Summary

This work presents a low-cost, open-source electrospinning system designed for nanofiber production. Built around an **Arduino Mega** and powered by **FreeRTOS**, the setup provides:

- Real-time task control and precision motor management
- High-voltage output regulation with feedback
- LCD-based parameter tuning
- Code hosted on GitHub with **GitHub Actions** CI/CD for cross-platform building

This modular, open-source system is intended to speed up research in **nanomaterials**, with potential applications in:
- Tissue engineering
- Filtration
- Energy storage

> “An open, real-time OS-based controller for electrospinning helps researchers tailor production conditions and explore novel materials.”

🧪 The work is part of research projects:
- Institutional subprogram **#02.04.02**: *Layered semiconductor compounds & nanostructures*
- Youth project **#24.80012.5007.12TC**
