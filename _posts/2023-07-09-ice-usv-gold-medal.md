### 🏆 Project Overview

**Title:** *Controlul inteligent al temperaturii de la distanță elaborat pentru tratamentul termic al foliilor de zinc și cupru*  
**Authors:** Cătălin Creciunel, Gheorghe Creciunel, Vladimir Ciobanu, Eduard Monaico  
**Institution:** National Center for Materials Study and Testing, Technical University of Moldova

---

### 🛠️ Technical Summary

The awarded project proposes a complete embedded solution for thermal control and monitoring of **zinc and copper foils** in nanostructure fabrication. Features include:

- **ESP32-based architecture** with galvanic isolation (optotriac + BT138)
- **FreeRTOS** for multitasking and timing accuracy
- Real-time temperature monitoring with PID regulation
- **Cross-platform interface** (Windows/Web/Android)
- Visual data tracking: temperature graph, process timing, user-defined setpoints

This system addresses the need for **precise, remotely managed thermal treatment**, crucial in nanomaterials research (e.g., ZnO synthesis).
