🏛️ **Location:** Tekwill, Chișinău  
📚 **Organizer:** Technical University of Moldova (FCIM)

---

### 🥈 Main Award – 2nd Place: **DevHealth Patient Monitor**

Developed in collaboration with **Daniel Țîrche**, under the guidance of **Prof. Adrian Bîrnaz**, the **DevHealth** system is a low-cost patient monitoring device.

**Technical Stack:**
* **Microcontrollers:** Dual-chip architecture with **STM32F411CE** (processing) and **ESP32** (connectivity).
* **Sensors:** Integrated ECG, SpO2, IR, and environmental gas sensors (MQ3, MQ7, MQ135).
* **Interface:** TFT screen with encoder navigation and a remote web-based medical dashboard.
* **Communication:** Optimized UART protocol for inter-chip data exchange.

> “DevHealth demonstrates how interdisciplinary engineering can create accessible medical solutions.”

### 🥇 Multiple Special Prizes

Our projects received significant recognition from industry leaders:

* 🏅 **Special Prize – AROBS Software SRL** *Awarded to DevHealth for innovation in embedded medical monitoring.*
* 🏅 **Special Prize – Societatea Absolvenților în Microelectronică UTM** *Awarded for the interdisciplinary design of the monitoring system.*
* 🏅 **2nd Special Prize – Mechatronics Innovation Center SRL** *Awarded to the **Temperature Control Device** (ESP32 + FreeRTOS).*
* 🏅 **Special Prize – MobileSoft Solutions SRL** *Recognizing the utility of the Temperature Control Device in industrial foil processing.*

---

### 🛠️ About the Temperature Control Device

Developed with **Gheorghe Creciunel** and coordinated by **Dr. Eduard Monaico**, this system was designed for precise thermal regulation of metal foils (Zn and Cu).

**Key Features:**
* **Safety:** 220V galvanic isolation with BT138 triac control.
* **Software:** Built on **FreeRTOS** for deterministic PID loop execution.
* **Connectivity:** Native support for Windows, Android, and Web interfaces.
* **Control:** Real-time PID stabilization with live status graphing.
